A Solutions Architect turns a business problem into a system that works on AWS. Most of the job is asking the right questions: how available it must be, how much data the business can afford to lose, where the data is allowed to live, and how much it may cost. The answers decide which services to use and how to connect them.

This course teaches that skill and maps it to the **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)** exam. Module 1 covers the role and how the exam's scenario questions work. Modules 2 through 5 follow the exam's four domains in order of weight: **secure** (30%), **resilient** (26%), **high-performing** (24%), and **cost-optimized** (20%). Module 6 puts it all together in a full design case study and a 30-day study plan.

Every lesson has a diagram, a common pitfall, a hands-on or whiteboard exercise, and a knowledge check written in the exam's style.

Exam details were checked against the official AWS exam guide in November 2026. AWS revises the exam guide from time to time, so check the current version before you book.
