This course applies the 4D AI Fluency Framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence), created by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller with Anthropic and released under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), to life-science work. All lesson content, examples, and exercises are original. Regulatory guidance on AI is changing quickly, and several documents referenced here were drafts when this course was written; always check the current version. This course is educational and is not legal, regulatory, or quality advice. Follow your organization's SOPs and your quality unit's direction.
