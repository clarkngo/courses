A Technical Account Manager (TAM) is the customer's technical advocate inside AWS. They are part engineer, part operations coach, part program manager, and part trusted advisor. The interview tests all four, through the lens of Amazon's Leadership Principles.

This course is organized around those four parts. You'll learn how the role fits into AWS Support and the account team, review the technical breadth interviewers probe, practice the incident, escalation, and proactive-guidance work TAMs do every week, and build the customer-facing judgment the role needs. The last two modules are about the interview itself: mapping your experience to the Leadership Principles, building a STAR story bank, rehearsing the scenarios a loop is likely to include, and following a 30-day plan.

Support plans, programs, and hiring steps change. Where a detail is time-sensitive, the lesson says so. Check the current AWS Support pages and the specific job posting before your interview.
