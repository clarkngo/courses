This course applies the 4D AI Fluency Framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence), created by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller with Anthropic and released under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), to agent-first development with Google Antigravity. All lesson content, examples, and exercises are original to this course. Learn more about the framework at [aifluencyframework.org](https://aifluencyframework.org/).

This is an independent course. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Antigravity changes fast, with new releases most weeks. Product names, settings, models, and plans described here reflect Google's documentation and changelog as of November 2026; check the [official Antigravity documentation](https://antigravity.google/docs) for current details. The habits this course teaches will outlast any single release.
