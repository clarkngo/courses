This course is inspired by the topics in Martin Kleppmann's *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (O'Reilly), which we recommend as a companion read. All lesson content, examples, diagrams, and exercises here are original. Specific products (PostgreSQL, Kafka, Cassandra, etcd and others) are mentioned only as examples of the ideas; check their current documentation for exact behavior.
