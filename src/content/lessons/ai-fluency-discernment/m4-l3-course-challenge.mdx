## How the Challenge Works

The challenge has two parts:

1. **Knowledge check:** ten scenario questions covering every module. Aim for at least 8 out of 10. If you miss one, read the explanation and revisit that lesson.
2. **Capstone task:** a full discernment review of a realistic AI-generated document, using the checklist you built in the previous lesson.

This course is free and self-paced, so there's nothing to submit. The goal is a review habit you'll actually keep.

## Part 2: Capstone Task

<TryIt title="A Full Discernment Review">

You'll ask an AI to produce a realistic, substantial document, then review it as if your name were going on it.

1. **Generate the document.** Choose a topic related to your work, studies, or community where facts can be checked publicly. Ask an AI assistant:
   ```text
   Write a 500-word briefing for [audience] on [topic]. Include at
   least six specific facts or statistics with sources, one
   recommendation with reasons, and one small table of figures.
   ```
2. **Set the level (Module 1).** Rate stakes, checkability, and reach as if this briefing were going to its real audience. Choose your rung.
3. **Check the product (Module 2).**
   - Extract and rank every specific claim.
   - Verify the top six against independent sources. Check that every citation exists *and* supports its claim.
   - Sanity-check the table's figures and totals.
   - Run a gap test: who's affected but not represented?
4. **Check the reasoning (Module 3).**
   - Surface the assumptions behind the recommendation and mark which are true for the real audience.
   - Get the strongest counter-argument in a new chat.
   - Recompute at least one figure in the table yourself.
5. **Check the performance.** Did the AI flag uncertainty? Did it agree too easily when you pushed back? Try one pushback on a claim you've verified as correct.
6. **Apply your checklist (Module 4).** Run your personal checklist from the previous lesson. Write the AI-use note you'd attach for a reviewer.
7. **Produce the corrected version.** Fix, flag, or remove every problem you found.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You've completed the capstone when you can say yes to all of these:

- I set a review level before I started, and can explain why.
- I verified every load-bearing claim against a source outside the AI.
- I checked at least one citation by reading the relevant part of the source.
- I identified at least one assumption or missing perspective the AI didn't mention.
- I tested at least one number or result myself.
- My corrected version has no unverified claims presented as fact.
- I'd put my name on the corrected version.

</TryIt>

## Congratulations, You've Finished Discernment: Judging AI Output

You can now:

- Explain why AI output sounds right even when it isn't: **hallucination**, **sycophancy**, and **overconfidence**
- **Calibrate** how much checking a piece of output deserves
- **Verify** facts, citations, and numbers against independent sources
- Spot **bias** and **missing perspectives** with swap and gap tests
- Judge **reasoning** by surfacing assumptions and stress-testing conclusions
- Check AI-generated **analysis, charts, and formulas** without needing to be technical
- Set up **team review practices** and maintain your own **discernment checklist**
