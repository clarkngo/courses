## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AGMA** | American Gear Manufacturers Association; publishes gear ratings and service factors |
| **Bar sag** | Indicator bracket droop that skews bottom readings |
| **Brinelling** | Raceway dents from impact or improper mounting force |
| **C3** | Bearing suffix: greater-than-normal internal clearance |
| **Elongation** | Chain "stretch" from pin and bushing wear |
| **Fluting** | Washboard raceway damage from electrical current |
| **L10 life** | Life that 90% of identical bearings reach or exceed |
| **Matched set** | Belts made to equal length for multi-belt drives |
| **Mil** | One thousandth of an inch (0.001 in) |
| **Offset / angular misalignment** | Displaced vs. angled shaft centerlines |
| **P-F interval** | Time from detectable defect to functional failure |
| **Pitch** | Distance between chain pins or sprocket/belt teeth |
| **QD / Taper-Lock** | Common tapered bushing styles |
| **Service factor** | Multiplier on load to size for real conditions |
| **Sheave** | A grooved pulley for V-belts |
| **Soft foot** | A machine foot that doesn't sit flat |
| **Spalling** | Flaking of raceway surface from fatigue |
| **Synchronous belt** | Toothed belt that doesn't slip |
| **Thermal growth** | Machine dimension change between cold and hot |

## Formulas to Remember

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Horsepower | hp = torque (lb-ft) × rpm ÷ 5,252 |
| Speed ratio | Driver speed ÷ driven speed = driven teeth (or diameter) ÷ driver teeth (or diameter) |
| Output torque | Input torque × ratio × efficiency |
| Bearing bore (codes 04+) | Bore code × 5 mm |
| Ball bearing L10 | (C ÷ P)³ × 1,000,000 revolutions |
| Belt deflection | Span (in) × 1/64 |
| Chain elongation | (Measured − nominal) ÷ nominal × 100 |
| Required gearbox rating | Load × service factor |
| Rim offset | Indicator reading ÷ 2 |

<TryIt title="Capstone: Drivetrain Inspection">

Choose one motor-driven machine with a belt or chain and a gearbox. With it locked out and guards removed by an authorized person, complete a one-page report:

1. **Nameplates:** motor hp and rpm; gearbox ratio. Calculate output torque.
2. **Belts or chain:** type and size; tension or slack; sheave or sprocket wear; alignment.
3. **Gearbox:** oil level for the mounting position, oil condition, breather type, leaks.
4. **Coupling:** type, condition, signs of misalignment (dust, heat, wear).
5. **Bearings:** numbers (if visible), greasing method, temperature trend.
6. **Guarding:** is every nip point and rotating part guarded?
7. **Recommendations:** ranked by risk.

</TryIt>
