## Category 7: Where the Points Are

Category 7 is worth **450 of 1,000 points**. Each results item mirrors process categories, so every approach you described should have results to show for it.

| Item | Points | What STC reports |
|---|---|---|
| **7.1 Student Learning and Process Results** | 120 | Program learning-outcome attainment; DFW rates; persistence and completion by segment; employment in field; certification pass rates built into courses; work-process measures (lab uptime, alert follow-up); continuity drill results; supplier performance |
| **7.2 Customer Results** | 80 | Student satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and engagement by segment; employer satisfaction and repeat hiring; complaint trends |
| **7.3 Workforce Results** | 80 | Capability and capacity (credentialed coverage, caseloads); engagement by segment; adjunct retention; development completion and effectiveness |
| **7.4 Leadership and Governance Results** | 80 | Two-way communication measures; accreditation and audit findings; accessibility-audit pass rate; integrity-case trends; societal contributions (clinic hours, pathway enrollments) |
| **7.5 Budgetary, Financial, Marketplace, and Strategy Results** | 90 | Net margin vs. budget; cost per credit hour; cloud spend per student; enrollment and market share; progress on each strategic objective |

## How Processes Are Scored: ADLI

Examiners score categories 1–6 on four factors:

| Factor | The question | STC example |
|---|---|---|
| **Approach** | Is there a systematic, repeatable method that fits your requirements? | Early-alert system with defined triggers, owners, and a 48-hour follow-up standard |
| **Deployment** | Is it used consistently across all relevant units, segments, and modalities? | Used in all online sections; *not yet* in partner-site cohorts |
| **Learning** | Is it evaluated and improved, with innovation and sharing? | Triggers refined twice using persistence data; best practice spread from one program to all |
| **Integration** | Is it aligned with strategy, measures, and other processes? | Directly supports SO2; feeds the monthly scorecard; linked to coach caseloads in Category 5 |

## How Results Are Scored: LeTCI

Examiners score Category 7 on four factors:

| Factor | The question |
|---|---|
| **Levels** | Where is performance now, on a meaningful scale? |
| **Trends** | Which direction is it moving, how fast, and how consistently (at least three data points)? |
| **Comparisons** | How does it compare with competitors, peers, or benchmarks? |
| **Integration** | Are these the results that matter (linked to key requirements, objectives, and segments), and do you use them in decisions? |

## Scoring Bands

Examiners place each item in a band, then pick a score within it. Here are the bands, paraphrased:

| Score | Processes (ADLI) | Results (LeTCI) |
|---|---|---|
| **0–5%** | No systematic approach; anecdotal | No results, or poor results; no trends or comparisons |
| **10–25%** | Beginning a systematic approach; early deployment; mostly reacting to problems | A few results; some early improvement; little comparative data |
| **30–45%** | Effective, systematic approach to the *basic* requirements; beginning systematic evaluation and improvement; early alignment | Good levels in a few areas; some beneficial trends; early comparisons |
| **50–65%** | Effective approach to the *overall* requirements; well deployed; fact-based improvement; aligned with key needs | Good levels and beneficial trends in many areas; some comparisons show good relative performance; most key requirements covered |
| **70–85%** | Responsive to *multiple* requirements; well deployed with no significant gaps; learning and innovation are key tools; integrated | Good to excellent levels in most areas; sustained trends; many comparisons show leadership; projections included |
| **90–100%** | Fully responsive and deployed; innovation through analysis and sharing everywhere; fully integrated | Excellent levels and sustained trends in most areas; benchmark leadership in many; fully covers key requirements |

The bands roughly match four **steps toward mature processes**: **reacting to problems** (0–25%), **early systematic approaches** (30–45%), **aligned approaches** (50–65%), and **integrated approaches** (70–100%).

## Two Worked Examples

**Process: STC's early-alert system (part of 3.2 and 6.1).**
- *Approach:* systematic, with defined triggers and a follow-up standard. Strong.
- *Deployment:* all online sections, but not partner-site cohorts. A gap.
- *Learning:* triggers refined twice using data; practice spread across programs. Good.
- *Integration:* linked to SO2, the scorecard, and coach caseloads. Good.
- **Band: 50–65%.** It's an effective, aligned approach with fact-based improvement. A deployment gap and no innovation yet keep it out of 70–85%.

**Result: online first-year persistence (7.1).**
- *Levels:* 73%, good. *Trends:* five years, improving every year. *Comparisons:* above peer median, below top quartile. *Integration:* tied to SO2, but no segment breakdown shown.
- **Band: 50–65%.** Good level, beneficial trend, and good relative performance against peers. To reach 70–85%, STC would need top-quartile comparisons and **segmented** results.

<Pitfall title="Results Without Comparisons or Segments">

**Common mistake:** STC's 7.1 shows 15 charts, each a single bar for this year, with no trend, no comparison, and no breakdown by segment.

A single data point can only show a **level**. Examiners need **three or more points** for a trend, a **comparison** from the sources named in P.2a(3), and **segments** that match the groups in P.1. Without those, even excellent performance scores low.

</Pitfall>

## Key Takeaway

Results are **45% of the score**, and 7.1 Student Learning and Process Results is the largest item. Processes are scored with **ADLI** (approach, deployment, learning, integration) and results with **LeTCI** (levels, trends, comparisons, integration). Use the **scoring bands** to see what the next level requires. Most gains come from closing **deployment gaps**, showing **trends**, adding **comparisons**, and **segmenting** results.

<TryIt title="Score One Process and One Result">

Pick one process and one result from your school.

1. For the **process**, write one sentence each for A, D, L, and I, then choose a band.
2. For the **result**, plot at least three years if you can. Write one sentence each for Le, T, C, and I, then choose a band.
3. For each, write the **single change** that would move it up one band.

Ask a colleague to score them independently, then compare. Differences show where your evidence is unclear.
