## The 2026 Revision

In August 2026, the Baldrige Foundation released a revised **Baldrige Excellence Framework** for business, nonprofit, and government organizations. The Foundation lists the **Education edition as coming in fall 2026**, and it hadn't been released when this course was written (November 2026). Until it is, the 2023–2024 Education edition you've studied is the current one for schools.

The main changes announced for the 2026 framework:

| Change | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Six categories** | Leadership, Strategy, Customers, **Organizational Intelligence**, Workforce, Operations. The framework and the award criteria now share this structure. |
| **Results built into every category** | There's no standalone Results category. Results are judged alongside the processes that produce them. |
| **Category 4 renamed** | "Measurement, Analysis, and Knowledge Management" becomes **Organizational Intelligence**. |
| **Maturity Pathways** | Three starting points, **Establishing**, **Developing**, and **Maturing**, so organizations can begin where they are. |
| **Artificial intelligence** | AI is addressed explicitly, along with the governance, security, and use of organizational data. |
| **Scoring aligned to the framework** | The scoring rubric is aligned directly with the categories. |
| **No edition years** | References to specific years were removed so the framework doesn't look dated. |
| **Updated language** | A revised glossary and more neutral, modern workforce terms. |

## The Maturity Pathways

| Pathway | Focus | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Establishing:** operational awareness | Shared understanding of how work is done, what customers and employees need, and which results matter | A shared picture of what the school is trying to do, and enough visibility to start disciplined improvement |
| **Developing:** process discipline | Consistent, reliable processes; clear roles; results with levels, trends, and some comparisons | Reliable processes improved with data; results getting better and more consistent |
| **Maturing:** systems thinking and integration | Alignment across leadership, strategy, people, and operations; routine cycles of improvement and innovation | Sustained high performance, intentional adaptation, often a role model for others |

The pathways describe the **organization**, but in practice different areas can be at different stages. STC's self-check:

| Area | STC's pathway today | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Developing | Objectives and action plans are in place; projections are new |
| Customers (students) | Developing | Lifecycle listening works; segmentation of results is uneven |
| Organizational Intelligence | Developing | Shared definitions exist; AI governance is new |
| Workforce | **Establishing** | Adjunct engagement drivers only identified this year |
| Operations | Developing | Master shells and in-process measures; continuity drills just started |
| Leadership | Developing | Leadership system defined; two-way channels still weak for adjuncts |

## Baldrige and NWCCU Accreditation

Schools in the Northwest are accredited by the **Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU)**. Its 2020 Standards emphasize continuous improvement and student achievement, which is where Baldrige is strongest. A Baldrige self-assessment produces much of the evidence an NWCCU report needs:

| Baldrige (2023–2024 Education) | NWCCU 2020 Standards (summarized) |
|---|---|
| **P.1** mission; **1.1** leadership system | **1.A** Institutional mission |
| **2** Strategy; **4.1** measurement and review | **1.B** Improving institutional effectiveness: continuous assessment, inclusive planning, peer comparison |
| **6.1** assess learning; **7.1** learning results | **1.C** Student learning: program outcomes, assessment, and use of results |
| **7.1** persistence, completion, and employment **by segment**, with **comparisons** | **1.D** Student achievement: disaggregated indicators compared with regional and national peers, published and used to close equity gaps |
| **1.2** governance, legal, and ethical behavior | **2.A** Governance; **2.D** Institutional integrity |
| **5** Workforce | **2.F** Human resources |
| **3.2** student support; **6.2** continuity | **2.G** Student support resources; **2.I** Physical and technological infrastructure |
| **7.5** financial results | **2.E** Financial resources |

The strongest link is **NWCCU 1.D**. It asks for student achievement indicators that are **disaggregated**, **compared with peers**, **published**, and **used to close gaps**. That's LeTCI applied to student success. For programs with **ABET** accreditation, ABET's continuous-improvement requirement fits naturally with the Learning factor in ADLI.

Check the official NWCCU Standards for exact wording before you cite them in a report.

Ways to get outside help and feedback:

- **Short self-assessment tools.** NIST's *Baldrige Excellence Builder* is a shorter version of the Criteria questions. Check the Baldrige Foundation for the current version.
- **Alliance for Performance Excellence programs.** State and regional Baldrige-based programs run award processes with examiner feedback. A Top Tier award from one is a standard pathway to the national award. Check whether one serves your state.
- **Train as an examiner.** Faculty and staff who serve as examiners for an Alliance program learn the framework fast and bring it home.
- **Learn from recipients.** Award recipients present at the Baldrige Foundation's **Quest for Excellence** conference, and recipient application summaries are a good model of how to write responses.

<Pitfall title="Waiting for the Perfect Edition">

**Common mistake:** "The 2026 Education edition is coming soon, so let's wait and start next year."

The questions that matter (*how do you do this, and how do you know it works?*) are the same in both editions. The Organizational Profile, scorecard, and improvement projects you build now carry straight over. Start with the 2023–2024 Education edition, and switch when the new one is released.

</Pitfall>

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ADLI** | Approach, Deployment, Learning, Integration: the factors for scoring processes |
| **Action plan** | Specific actions, with owners, resources, and timelines, that carry out a strategic objective |
| **Core competencies** | What you do best, and what your mission depends on |
| **Engagement** | Commitment to the mission; for students and customers, willingness to recommend and return |
| **In-process measure** | A measure taken during a process that predicts the outcome |
| **Key work processes** | The few processes that create value for students and other customers directly |
| **LeTCI** | Levels, Trends, Comparisons, Integration: the factors for scoring results |
| **Maturity Pathways** | 2026 framework: Establishing, Developing, Maturing |
| **OFI** | Opportunity for improvement, as given in a feedback report |
| **Organizational Intelligence** | 2026 name for the measurement, analysis, and knowledge category |
| **Organizational Profile** | A snapshot of your organization, its environment, relationships, and strategic situation |
| **Segment** | A part of your student, customer, or workforce base with distinct needs |
| **Strategic advantages / challenges** | Factors that strongly help or threaten your future success |
| **Strategic objective** | An outcome-based goal, with a target and date, that responds to strategic challenges |
| **Supply network** | The external organizations that help produce and deliver your programs and services |
| **Systematic** | Well-ordered, repeatable, and using data for learning and improvement |

## Key Takeaway

The **2026 revision** moves to **six categories**, builds **results into each**, renames Category 4 **Organizational Intelligence**, adds three **Maturity Pathways**, and addresses **AI** explicitly. The Education edition is due in fall 2026, so start now with 2023–2024. Your work carries over. Baldrige maps closely to **NWCCU**, especially **1.D's disaggregated, peer-compared student achievement indicators**. Follow a **12-month cycle** (profile, assess, choose, improve, reassess) and use Alliance programs and examiner training for outside feedback.
