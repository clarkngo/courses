## Pick Just One

There are several good AI assistants, and they're more alike than different. Don't worry about choosing the "best" one. Choose one, get comfortable, and you can always try another later.

| Assistant | Made by | Good choice if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini** | Google | You use Gmail or an Android phone. Sign in with your Google account. |
| **ChatGPT** | OpenAI | You want the most widely used option. Many family members may already know it. |
| **Copilot** | Microsoft | You use a Windows computer or Outlook email. |
| **Claude** | Anthropic | You often want help with long letters or documents. |

All four have a **free version**. You do not need to pay for anything in this course.

> **Family helpers:** if you're setting this up for a parent or friend, choose the assistant that matches the account they already use most (Google, Microsoft, or Apple). One fewer password to remember makes a big difference.

On a computer, you can skip the app and just visit the website instead:

- Gemini: **gemini.google.com**
- ChatGPT: **chatgpt.com**
- Copilot: **copilot.microsoft.com**
- Claude: **claude.ai**

Type the address carefully into the bar at the top of your browser, then bookmark it so you never have to type it again.

<Pitfall title="Signing In Through a Link in an Email or Text">

**Common mistake:** clicking a "Get started with AI" link that arrived in an email or text message.

Scammers send fake "AI upgrade" and "activate your AI assistant" messages that lead to look-alike sign-in pages. **Always go to the app store or type the web address yourself.** No real AI company will text you asking you to sign in.

</Pitfall>

## Two Privacy Settings Worth Changing

Once you're signed in, take two minutes to look at the settings. The exact wording changes from time to time, but look for these:

1. **"Improve the model for everyone" / "Help improve" / "Training."** When this is on, your conversations may be used to train future versions of the AI. If you'd rather keep your chats out of that, turn it **off**.
2. **Chat history / Activity.** This controls whether your past conversations are saved. Saved history is handy (you can find an old recipe or letter later), but you can delete individual chats or turn it off.

If you can't find these settings, ask the AI itself: *"How do I turn off training on my conversations in this app?"* Then check that the steps match what you see on screen.

## Make It Easier to Read and Hear

AI assistants work well with the accessibility features already on your device:

- **Bigger text.** On a phone, go to *Settings → Display* (or *Accessibility*) → *Text size*. On a computer, hold **Ctrl** (or **Cmd** on a Mac) and press **+** to zoom in.
- **Talk instead of type.** Tap the **microphone** 🎤 icon in the chat box to speak your question. Many apps also have a **voice conversation** mode (often a sound-wave icon) where you can talk back and forth out loud.
- **Have it read aloud.** Look for a **speaker** 🔊 icon next to the answer, or ask: *"Please keep your answers short so they're easy to listen to."*
- **Ask for simpler formatting.** You can always say, *"Please use large, short paragraphs and no tables."*
