## How the Challenge Works

The challenge has two parts:

1. **Knowledge check** — ten scenario questions covering every module. Aim for at least 8 out of 10. If you miss one, read the explanation and revisit that lesson.
2. **Capstone task** — a hands-on exercise that uses the full workflow: collaborate, prompt with CRAFT, chain, verify, and use AI responsibly.

This course is free and self-paced, so there's no grade to submit. The goal is simple: finish able to use AI confidently, effectively, and responsibly in your own work.

## Part 2: Capstone Task

<TryIt title="From Blank Page to Finished, Verified Work">

Choose one **real** deliverable you need in the next month — a report, a proposal, a training guide, a process document, a presentation, a newsletter. Then work through the full workflow:

1. **Collaborate (Module 1).** Start by asking the AI to ask you 5 questions about the task before it does anything.
2. **Set up safely (Module 2).** Before pasting anything, decide what's safe to share. Remove or replace sensitive details.
3. **Know the limits (Module 3).** List which parts of the deliverable depend on facts the AI can't know or might get wrong. Plan to supply or verify those.
4. **Prompt with CRAFT (Module 4).** Write a full Context–Role–Ask–Format prompt. Include "use placeholders instead of inventing facts."
5. **Chain it (Module 5).** Work in steps: outline → draft sections → critique from the audience's point of view → revise. Create one visual if it helps. If the chat gets long, use a handoff summary.
6. **Take responsibility (Module 6).** Check every fact and placeholder against real sources. Review for bias and tone. Decide how you'll disclose AI assistance, if needed.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You've completed the capstone when you can say yes to all of these:

- The final piece is something I'd confidently put my name on.
- I verified every factual claim against a source outside the AI.
- I didn't share anything I shouldn't have.
- I can point to at least two places where *my* judgment changed the AI's output for the better.
- I saved at least one reusable prompt template to my prompt library.

You can now:

- Work with AI as a **collaborator**, not a vending machine

- **Chain prompts**, carry context between chats, and **generate visuals**
