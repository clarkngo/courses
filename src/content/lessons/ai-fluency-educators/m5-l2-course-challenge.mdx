## How the Challenge Works

1. **Knowledge check:** ten classroom scenarios covering every module. Aim for at least 8 out of 10, and revisit any lesson where you missed a question.

## Part 2: Capstone

<TryIt title="Finalize Your Policy and Unit Plan">

Bring together your work from Modules 4 and 5 into two documents you can use this term.

1. **Course AI policy** (from Lesson 4.1), finalized to include:
   - Permission levels and where students find each assignment's level
   - Reasons tied to learning
   - Disclosure expectations and an AI use statement template
   - Privacy guidance and approved tools
   - A fair, conversation-first integrity process (from Lesson 4.2)
   - An invitation to ask questions
2. **AI-fluent unit plan** (from Lesson 5.1), finalized to include:
   - Your own AI-supported prep tasks, with the discernment checks you'll run
   - Every assignment's purpose, AI level, and reason
   - At least one description activity and one discernment activity for students
   - Two modeling moments
   - A note on how you'll protect student data
3. **Get a second opinion.** Share both with a colleague, coach, or (de-identified) an AI assistant acting as a skeptical department head. Revise.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You're done when you can say yes to all of these:

- Every assignment in the unit has a purpose, an AI level, and a reason.
- No student data goes into a tool that isn't approved for it.
- I read and judge student work before any AI is involved in feedback.
- My integrity approach doesn't depend on a detector score.
- Students will see me use AI, including checking it and deciding not to use it.
- I'd be comfortable showing this plan to my students' families.

</TryIt>

## Congratulations, You've Finished AI Fluency for Educators

You can now:

- Use AI fluently in your own **planning, materials, and feedback**, with student privacy protected
- Decide what to **hand off and what to keep** in teaching work
- Teach students to **delegate, describe, discern, and practice diligence** through short, subject-based activities
- Write a clear, **assignment-level AI policy** with reasons students understand
- Handle **academic integrity** fairly, without relying on detectors
- **Model** fluent AI use for your learners
