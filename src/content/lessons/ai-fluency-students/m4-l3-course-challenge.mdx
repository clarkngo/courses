## Part 2: Capstone

<TryIt title="One Assignment, Done Fluently">

Choose a real upcoming assignment where your course allows at least some AI use, or create a realistic practice one (for example, a one-page argument on a topic you're studying).

1. **Check the rules (Lesson 1.2).** Write down the AI level or policy. If it's unclear, ask your teacher.
2. **Decide the role (Lesson 1.1).** List what you'll use AI for and what you'll do yourself, and why. Apply the question: *does this help me learn, or skip it?*
3. **Study and research (Module 2).** Use AI to quiz you or explain background. Find and read real sources yourself. Verify any source AI suggests.
4. **Do the work (Module 3).** Brainstorm with AI if allowed, then outline and draft yourself. Get "point out, don't fix" feedback and revise yourself.
5. **Keep your record (Lesson 4.1).** Use a tool with version history and save your notes and sources.
6. **Disclose (Lesson 4.1).** Write an AI use statement: tool, uses, what you did yourself, how you checked.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You're done when you can say yes to all of these:

- I followed my course's AI rules, and asked when unsure.
- I could explain every part of this work without AI's help.
- Every source I cited is one I found and read myself.
- The writing or solution is in my own words and reflects my own thinking.
- I have a record of how the work developed.
- My AI use statement is accurate and complete.

</TryIt>

## Congratulations, You've Finished AI Fluency for Students

You can now:

- Tell when AI **helps your learning** and when it **skips it**
- Read your courses' AI rules, **ask when unsure**, and protect your privacy
- Use AI as a **tutor**: explanations at your level, retrieval quizzes, teach-backs, and spaced review
- Read and research with AI **without fake sources**
- Get help on **writing, problems, and code** while keeping the work your own
- **Disclose honestly**, keep a record of your process, and respond calmly to concerns
- Connect your AI habits to the **skills employers value**
