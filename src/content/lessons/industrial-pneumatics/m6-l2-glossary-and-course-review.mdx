## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **3/2, 5/2, 5/3** | Valve naming: ports / positions |
| **Coalescing filter** | Removes fine oil aerosols and water droplets |
| **Compression ratio** | (gauge pressure + 14.7) ÷ 14.7 |
| **Cushion** | Trapped exhaust air that slows the piston near end of stroke |
| **Double-acting** | Cylinder driven by air in both directions |
| **Double-solenoid valve** | Valve with "memory": stays in its last shifted position |
| **Dump valve** | Lockable 3/2 valve that blocks supply and exhausts downstream air |
| **Duty cycle** | Percent of time a compressor can run loaded without overheating |
| **FRL** | Filter, regulator, lubricator at the machine |
| **Load/unload test** | Measures leakage: T ÷ (T + t) with production off |
| **Meter-out** | Restricting exhaust air to control cylinder speed |
| **NC / NO** | Normally closed / normally open valve |
| **PDP** | Pressure dew point: temperature where water condenses at line pressure |
| **Pilot-operated** | Valve whose main spool is shifted by pilot air |
| **psig / psia** | Gauge pressure / absolute pressure (gauge + ~14.7) |
| **Quick exhaust valve** | Dumps cylinder air directly at the port for speed |
| **Receiver** | Storage tank that buffers demand and drops out water |
| **scfm** | Flow in standard cubic feet per minute |
| **Side load** | Force across the rod; destroys bushings and seals |
| **Soft-start valve** | Ramps up pressure gradually when air is restored |
| **Venturi / ejector** | Makes vacuum from a compressed air jet |
| **VSD** | Variable speed drive compressor that matches output to demand |

## Formulas to Remember

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cylinder extend force | Pressure × π × (bore ÷ 2)² |
| Cylinder retract force | Pressure × (piston area − rod area) |
| Absolute pressure | Gauge + 14.7 psi (at sea level) |
| Vacuum cup force | Vacuum (psi) × cup area ÷ safety factor |
| Leakage % | Loaded time ÷ (loaded + unloaded time) × 100 |
| Leak cost | scfm × kW/scfm × hours × $/kWh |

<TryIt title="Capstone: Walk Down a Machine">

Choose a pneumatic machine in your workplace (or a video of one). Produce a one-page walkdown:

1. **Supply:** Where does its drop come off the header? What pressure does the FRL regulate to? Is there a lockable **dump** valve?
2. **Valves:** How many? Single or double solenoid? Any 5/3 valves, and what center?
3. **Actuators:** List each cylinder's bore and estimate its extend force at the set pressure.
4. **Speed control:** Meter-in or meter-out? Mounted at the cylinder?
5. **Leaks:** Listen or use soapy water at couplings, FRLs, and exhausts. Estimate leak sizes.
6. **Stored energy:** List every place energy remains after isolation.

You now have a maintenance baseline, a leak list, and the start of a lockout procedure.
