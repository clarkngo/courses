## How the Challenge Works

The challenge has two parts:

1. **Knowledge check:** ten scenario questions covering every module. Aim for at least 8 out of 10. If you miss one, read the explanation and revisit that lesson.
2. **Capstone task:** a hands-on exercise that takes a real piece of your own work through all four competencies.

This course is free and self-paced, so there's nothing to submit. The goal is to leave with a way of working with AI that you can apply to tools that don't exist yet.

## Part 2: Capstone Task

<TryIt title="Take Real Work Through All Four Ds">

Choose one **real** task you need to complete in the next few weeks, something with enough substance that you'd care if it went wrong. Work through it with an AI assistant, documenting each stage in a short write-up (bullet points are fine).

1. **Delegation (Module 2).**
   - Write the real goal and what success looks like.
   - Break the task into pieces and mark each **You**, **Augment**, or **Automate**, using the five-question delegation check.
   - Confirm the tool you're using is allowed for the data involved.
2. **Description (Module 3).**
   - For the pieces AI will help with, write a description covering **product**, **process**, and **performance**.
   - Use at least one technique: an example, a boundary, placeholders, or letting the AI interview you.
3. **Discernment (Module 4).**
   - Run the description–discernment loop at least three rounds. Record what you noticed, what caused it, and the feedback you gave.
   - Apply your personal discernment checklist to the final output. Verify every specific fact against a source outside the AI.
4. **Diligence (Module 5).**
   - Decide what disclosure, if any, is appropriate, and why.
   - Confirm you'd put your name on the result.
   - Check that the work is consistent with your personal AI use statement. Update the statement if you learned something.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You've completed the capstone when you can say yes to all of these:

- I kept at least one piece of this work for myself, on purpose, and can explain why.
- My description covered product, process, *and* performance.
- I can point to at least two places where my discernment changed the result for the better.
- I verified every factual claim against a source outside the AI.
- I didn't share anything I shouldn't have, and I handled disclosure deliberately.
- I'd confidently put my name on the final result.

</TryIt>

## Congratulations, You've Finished AI Fluency: Foundations

You can now:

- Explain what AI fluency is and why it outlasts any single tool or trick
- Recognize **automation**, **augmentation**, and **agency**, and choose the right mode
- **Delegate** deliberately, piece by piece, based on your goal and your tools
- **Describe** the product, process, and performance you need
- **Discern** the quality of AI output, reasoning, and behavior, and turn that into better descriptions
- Practice **diligence** in how you create, disclose, and deploy AI-assisted work
