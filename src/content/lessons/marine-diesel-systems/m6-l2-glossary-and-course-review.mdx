## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Azimuth drive** | Propulsion unit that rotates 360° to steer and thrust |
| **BDN** | Bunker delivery note: fuel quantity, density, sulfur |
| **Boost** | Charge air pressure from the turbocharger |
| **Bunkering** | Taking fuel aboard |
| **Charge air cooler** | Cools compressed intake air to increase density |
| **Common rail** | High-pressure fuel rail with electronic injectors |
| **CPP** | Controllable-pitch propeller |
| **ECA** | Emission Control Area (0.10% sulfur; Tier III NOx for newer ships) |
| **Keel cooler** | External hull-mounted coolant heat exchanger |
| **Lash** | Valve clearance |
| **MARPOL** | International convention on pollution from ships |
| **MGO / HFO / VLSFO** | Marine gas oil / heavy fuel oil / very low sulfur fuel oil |
| **Oil Record Book** | Required log of oil transfers, discharges, sludge |
| **OWS** | Oily water separator (15 ppm discharge limit) |
| **PMS** | Planned maintenance system |
| **Purifier** | Centrifugal separator removing water and solids from fuel or oil |
| **Raw water** | Seawater used for cooling |
| **SCR** | Selective catalytic reduction (urea + catalyst for NOx) |
| **TBN** | Total Base Number: oil's acid-neutralizing reserve |
| **TDC** | Top dead center |
| **Thrust bearing** | Transfers propeller thrust to the hull |
| **Turning gear** | Slowly rotates the engine for maintenance; interlocked against starting |

## Quick Diagnostic Reference

| Symptom | First checks |
|---|---|
| Won't start | Fuel (air in lines), cranking speed, air intake, compression |
| Black smoke | Air supply, overload, injectors, hull/prop fouling |
| Blue smoke | Oil burning: rings, guides, turbo seals |
| White smoke | Cold, water in fuel, low compression, coolant leak |
| Overheating | Raw water flow, strainer, impeller, coolant level, heat exchanger |
| Low oil pressure | Stop or reduce load; level, dilution, filter, pump |
| Power loss + vibration | Propeller fouling or damage |
