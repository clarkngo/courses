## Part 2: Capstone

<TryIt title="Build Your Team AI Fluency Package">

Bring together the work from every module into one package you can use with your team.

1. **Current state (Module 1):** your AI use snapshot summary and where your team sits on the Scattered → Visible → Shared → Fluent path.
2. **Workflow maps (Lesson 1.2):** at least two key workflows, with AI fit, named checkpoints, and protected skill-building steps.
3. **Shared practice (Module 2):** at least one tested shared instruction with a definition of done, plus your review tiers.
4. **Governance (Module 3):** your one-page team AI policy, checked by the right people in your organization, and a fairness check for any AI use that affects decisions about people.
5. **Rollout (Module 4):** your pilot design and your 90-day plan with owners and dates.

**Self-assessment checklist.** You're done when you can say yes to all of these:

- My team knows which tools are approved and what data can go where.
- Every AI-assisted workflow has named human checkpoints at the right review tier.
- Decisions about people stay with accountable humans.
- We have at least one shared, owned, and dated instruction.
- My pilot measures quality as well as speed, and has a stop condition.
- People on my team know it's safe to raise AI concerns and report mistakes.

</TryIt>

## Congratulations, You've Finished AI Fluency for Leaders & Teams

You can now:

- Move a team from **scattered** AI use to **shared, accountable** practice
- **Map workflows** to place AI deliberately and protect skill development
- Build **shared instructions** and **definitions of done**
- Set **risk-tiered review**, counter automation bias, and run **blameless reviews**
- Write a practical **team AI policy** within organizational and legal rules
- Keep **decisions about people** human and check AI for **fairness**
- Lead adoption through **pilots, champions, honest communication**, and **outcome measures**

### Where to Go Next

- **Get your whole team on the same page:** [AI Fluency: Foundations](/courses/ai-fluency-foundations/) gives everyone a shared vocabulary.
- **Strengthen your review practices:** [Discernment: Judging AI Output](/courses/ai-fluency-discernment/) covers verification, bias, and team review techniques in depth.
- **Think in systems:** [Human-Centered Systems Thinking](/courses/human-centered-systems-thinking/) helps you see how AI changes your organization as a whole.
